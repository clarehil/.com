Our Products

Through our skill-acquisition and empowerment initiatives, Clarehil Literacy Development Foundation equips young girls and women with practical, income-generating skills. These products represent more than what we make they represent skills acquired, confidence built, and opportunities created for sustainable livelihoods.

PERFUMES

Beautifully crafted fragrances produced through our skill-acquisition and entrepreneurship programme. Through perfume-making training, young women and girls gain practical skills that can be developed into sustainable income-generating ventures.

GERMICIDE

A hygiene-focused product developed through our empowerment initiative, where young women are trained in practical production skills. It supports everyday cleanliness and hygiene while creating opportunities for economic independence.

Liquid Soap

Quality liquid soap produced through our women and girls’ skill-acquisition programme, equipping beneficiaries with practical skills for self-reliance and sustainable livelihoods. Suitable for everyday household cleaning and personal hygiene.

Perfumes

Beautifully crafted fragrances created through our skill-acquisition and entrepreneurship programme. Each perfume represents creativity, practical learning, and an opportunity for young women and girls to develop valuable skills that can grow into sustainable income-generating ventures.
